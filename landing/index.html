/Features in flux

Special LR2021 features. I learned a lot reverse engineering the LR2021, so nodes with this chip get special treatment. The research ↗

Eavesdrop on the other network. LR2021 only Type mt on to scan both frequencies. A custom PRAM patch does this entirely in the LoRa chip, so the MCU can rest. 😴 Meshtastic

Weather stations. LR2021 only Type wx on and readings from nearby Ecowitt, Ambient, La Crosse and ThermoPro sensors show up in their own channel. Even your neighbor’s. Weather

Planes overhead. LR2021 only Type air on to listen for ADS-B on 978 and 1090 MHz. Every 10 minutes you get a list of the planes nearby, with their tail number or flight, altitude and how far away they are. Or watch them live on a map in the app, over Bluetooth. Aircraft

Time-of-flight ranging. LR2021 only Find the distance between two nodes without GPS. Find me

Share a key between radios. Normally two radios with the same key break DMs, but fluxcore detects when it hears its clone and asks if it should take over as the primary. Takeover

Little things that bugged me. Messages that don’t make it out get sent once more. Plugging a radio in to charge doesn’t turn it on anymore (on most boards). The buzzer, vibration and LEDs each get their own off switch. And you can type commands like /path right in a chat. The rest

Also: a radar sweep detector, packet parser fixes, improved key generation, radio diagnostics, and opt-in experimental encryption. All the changes.
